# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what Companion Atlas's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute Companion Atlas source code for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

Companion Atlas is a 3D XR memory globe for reviewing and planning your travels, built on your Companion Memory data.

Companion Atlas uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion Atlas user?

If you use Companion Atlas for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork Companion Atlas?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute Companion Atlas under the same license.

### Can I contribute to Companion Atlas?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell Companion Atlas?

No. Selling Companion Atlas, including cloud-hosted instances or paid support for those products, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Most other Companion Intelligence product repositories use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories — see the org's open-source rollout tracker for the current list.

### Unsure whether your use case is allowed?

Email support@companionintelligence.com.
